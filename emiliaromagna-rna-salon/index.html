About us

In recent years, insights into the complex life of RNA have revolutionized basic research and unlocked transformative applications across multiple fields, particularly in understanding and treating human disease.

The Emilia-Romagna (ER) Salon is meant to bring together the regional community of scientists passionate about RNA Biology.

Leveraging the unique infrastructure of local research institutions his hub aims to stimulate discussion and build synergies to bridge the gap between basic discovery and clinical applications in the field of RNA biology.

We hold meetings in an variety of formats, in person and online, with the aim of creating a favorable environment serving as an incubator that accelerates ideas, projects and innovation
